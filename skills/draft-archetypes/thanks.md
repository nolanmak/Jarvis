# Archetype: Thanks — no action needed

## Intent

The sender delivered something (a doc, an answer, a favor) or sent a kind
note, and the right reply is a warm, brief acknowledgment that closes the loop
without creating new work for anyone. Be specific about what you're thanking
them for so it doesn't read as a reflex. One or two sentences. No new asks.

## Exemplars (in the user's voice)

> Got it — thanks for turning {delivered_thing} around so fast. This is exactly
> what I needed.

> Really appreciate this, {sender_first_name}. {one_specific_detail}. Thank
> you.

> Thanks so much — that means a lot. Nothing needed on your end; just wanted to
> say I'm grateful.

## Slot hints

- `{delivered_thing}` — the specific doc/answer/favor received
- `{sender_first_name}` — use if known
- `{one_specific_detail}` — one concrete thing that made it useful/kind
