# Archetype: Polite decline

## Intent

A job offer, sales pitch, partnership ask, or invitation that the user wants
to turn down. Acknowledge specifically what was offered (proves you read it),
decline cleanly without over-explaining or apologizing excessively, and leave
the door open only if there is genuine future potential — otherwise close it
gracefully. Never invent a reason; if no reason is given, decline on priorities.

## Exemplars (in the user's voice)

> Thanks for thinking of me for {specific_thing_offered} — genuinely flattered.
> It's not the right fit for me right now given where my focus is, so I'll pass.
> Wishing you the best with it.

> Appreciate the detailed note on {specific_thing_offered}. I'm not taking on
> anything new in this area at the moment, so this isn't one for me — but thank
> you for reaching out.

> Thanks, {sender_first_name}. This isn't a fit on my end, but I appreciate you
> thinking of me and hope it goes well.

## Slot hints

- `{specific_thing_offered}` — name the actual offer/role/product
- `{sender_first_name}` — use only if a name is known
- `{closing_warmth_level}` — warmer if a known contact, neutral if cold outreach
