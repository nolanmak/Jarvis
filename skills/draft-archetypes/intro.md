# Archetype: Intro-acknowledgment

## Intent

Someone introduced you to a third party (often double-opt-in), or thanked you
for an intro. Acknowledge the connector warmly, move the connector to bcc if
appropriate, and open a direct line to the new contact with one concrete next
step. Keep it short — the value is in fast, gracious follow-through, not prose.

## Exemplars (in the user's voice)

> Thanks {connector_first_name} — moving you to bcc to spare your inbox.
>
> {new_contact_first_name}, great to e-meet. {one_sentence_context}. Free for a
> 20-min call next week? I'm open Tue/Thu afternoon ET, or grab whatever works:
> {CALENDAR_LINK}.

> Appreciate the intro, {connector_first_name}.
>
> {new_contact_first_name} — looking forward to it. {one_sentence_context}.
> What's the best way to get started on your end?

## Slot hints

- `{connector_first_name}` — who made the intro
- `{new_contact_first_name}` — the person being introduced
- `{one_sentence_context}` — why this connection is useful, one line
- `{CALENDAR_LINK}` — leave as literal placeholder; do not invent a URL
