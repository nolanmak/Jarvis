## Platform: LinkedIn

- 1–3 short paragraphs. First line is a standalone hook (LinkedIn truncates
  after ~210 chars behind "…see more" — earn the click).
- Professional but human. Specific > inspirational. No "Agree?" / "Thoughts?"
  engagement-bait closers, no humble-brag framing.
- One concrete takeaway the reader can use. Whitespace between paragraphs.
- 0–3 hashtags on their own final line, only if genuinely topical.
- Output: the post text only.
